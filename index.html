Production stages

How we work

The production plan and crew are defined after the brief and venue assessment.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Date, location, event format, venue, screens and broadcast platforms.

  2. 02

    Estimate

    Equipment, crew, scope of work and project cost.

  3. 03

    Technical plan

    Camera positions, signal routing, graphics and running order.

  4. 04

    Setup & rehearsal

    Equipment setup and checks for internet, sound, cameras and content.

  5. 05

    Filming & broadcast

    Event filming, live direction, venue screens and online delivery.

  6. 06

    Files & post-production

    Event recording on the same day. Highlight and promotional videos after the event.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do you need for an estimate?

Date, city, venue, event format, number of cameras, screens, streaming platform and required deliverables.

What determines the cost?

The number of cameras and screens, crew size, venue, internet, graphics, broadcast platform and post-production.

When do we receive the recording?

The event recording is delivered on the same day. Edited highlight and promotional videos are delivered to the agreed schedule.

Can you organise paid access to a stream?

Yes. We produce ticketed and Pay-Per-View broadcasts with individual access for each viewer.
